HOW TO PLAY

  • Green light: run toward the finish line. The doll faces away.
  • Red light: the doll turns and her eyes glow. Stop immediately. Any movement after a short reaction window eliminates you.
  • Reach the finish line (100 m) before the 70 second timer hits zero.
  • Sprinting is faster, but you need to stop just as quickly.

Controls

Desktop: WASD / Arrow keys to move, Shift to sprint, Space to jump, mouse to look (click the game to capture the mouse).
Mobile: left joystick to move, hold SPRINT to run, tap JUMP to jump, drag the right side to look around.
